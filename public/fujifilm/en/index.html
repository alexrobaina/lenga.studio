About me

I'm a photographer. For 11 years I worked in advertising and fashion in Buenos Aires, shooting campaigns and productions for brands.

Today I live in Mendoza and I'm training to become a mountain guide: I'm in the first year of the trekking guide program at EPGAMT, the provincial mountain guide school. I work seasonally in different places across Argentina. For the next four months I'm living in El Chaltén, at the foot of Mount Fitz Roy and Cerro Torre. Then I go back to Mendoza to keep studying and working.

That change led me to what I love most: landscape and mountain photography. From advertising I bring the craft of producing and delivering to campaign standards. The mountains gave me the subject.
